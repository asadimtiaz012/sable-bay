A shoreline built for stillness

Twelve rooms, one horizon, and the sound of water doing all the talking.

Sable Bay sits where the palms thin out and the sand goes quiet — a short walk of open coastline, a reef that keeps the swell honest, and mornings that start with the tide instead of a clock.

There's no itinerary here. Just warm sand underfoot, salt in the air, and enough space between rooms that the loudest thing you'll hear is the surf.

Three ways the coast keeps you here

Each stay is shaped around the water — how it moves, when it glows, and where it's calmest.

Private coves

Follow the palm line past the last suite and the beach empties out entirely — half a kilometre of sand that rarely sees another footprint.

Golden hour, every evening

The terrace faces due west. Around six, the whole coastline turns amber, and the bar starts pouring before anyone asks.

Ocean-edge suites

Every room opens straight onto the water — no lobby to cross, no path to follow. Just a door, a deck, and the sea.
